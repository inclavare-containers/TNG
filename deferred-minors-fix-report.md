## Fix 3 — SKIPPED (Rego dedup)
The two Rego templates are NOT byte-identical and could not be deduplicated
without changing generated bytes:
- Branch-A (`build_transparency_log_policy`) hardware block carries per-rule
  comments (`# Score 126 ...`), blank lines between rules, and a *duplicate*
  `hardware := 2` rule (top + bottom). Branch-B condenses the same rules with no
  comments, no blank lines, and no duplicate.
- The `actual_measurement`/`image_repo_name` blocks also differ: Branch-A carries
  `# extract ...` and `# repo name ...` comment lines; Branch-B omits them.
A shared `const &str` would change the generated Rego for one or both branches and
break the `transparency_log_rego_*` / `artifact_server_primary_*` content
assertions. Per the task's escape hatch, Fix 3 is skipped and the duplication
remains. No build/test breakage.
